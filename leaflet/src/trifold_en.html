About Yusando

We started our tea fields from zero in 2015, on the Yamato Plateau in Nara. Our tea is grown by Natural Agriculture: pesticide-free and fertilizer-free.

As neighbouring farmers gave up conventional tea farming, we took over their fields and converted them one by one. In more than 11 years, we have converted 3.5 hectares to Natural Agriculture.

What is Natural Agriculture?

No fertilizer at all, not even organic fertilizer, and no pesticides. Only the soil and the tea plant’s own vitality, given time to grow slowly.
